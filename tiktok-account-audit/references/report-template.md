# Account Audit Report Pattern

Use only sections that help the requested decision. Keep conclusion and action before detailed evidence.

## 1. Diagnosis and confidence

- One-sentence diagnosis:
- Account type and stage:
- Main opportunity/constraint:
- Confidence: A/B/C and reason.

## 2. Three immediate actions

| Priority | Action | Why now | Metric | Review/stop condition | Evidence/limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Evidence scope and source ledger

- Original/normalized URL and Handle.
- Requested/default and actual sample, window, pinned/recent handling, pages, completeness, and stop reason.
- Source ledger from the workflow.

For a reusable or partner-facing run, apply the shared [run evidence display](../../tiktok-shop-growth-research/references/evidence-display.md). Keep public-page observations, MCP observations, calculations, judgments, and actions visibly distinct.

## 4. Strengths, gaps, and account type

| Dimension | Observation | Evidence | Counterevidence/gap | Judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Content pillars and cadence

| Pillar | Sample count/share | Representative videos | Audience need | Evidence limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Call frequency “sample cadence” unless the collected window is complete.

## 6. Representative-video comparison

Keep recent, pinned, high-performing, low-performing, and commerce evidence labelled. Compare only compatible fields and formulas.

| Video/role | Date | Views/engagement | Commerce field | Hook/structure/CTA | Caption state | Why selected |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Repeated operating logic

| Pattern | At least two sources/examples | Counterexample/limit | Transferable condition | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 8. Reusable launch process

Label this as a derived plan, not private-history reconstruction.

| Stage | Goal | Content/operating action | Evidence/assumption | Metric | Adjust/stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Future seven days

| Day/phase | Action | Owner/input | Observable metric | Review decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 10. Missing information and impact

List unavailable, not public, failed, or incompatible fields; affected conclusions; attempted remedies; and the smallest useful next input.
